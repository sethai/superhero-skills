## Search and read

Use live search and open originals. Search snippets only identify leads. Aim for 5-7 relevant sources and about 20 web operations in quick mode, counting each query, open and find including failures. Do not pad to reach a count. If access failures consume the plan, change route promptly. Record the actual total and reason for any expansion. In deep mode, extend coverage where it changes the answer.

Read methods, results and limitations for evidence central to your answer. If an abstract is all you can access, say so and avoid claims needing unseen details. Check newer versions and follow-ups. Deduplicate the underlying study across its versions and retellings.

For fast-changing domains, follow the quality rubric's freshness check instead of relying on the broad default date window. Search the most recent applicable primary evidence first, and record when the data were collected and which tool generation/workflow was studied. Distinguish historical results and mechanisms from evidence applicable to current tools; a new publication date alone is insufficient. Report a lack of current direct evidence rather than filling that gap with older results.

Deliberately search for adverse/null results, rival explanations and boundary conditions. Give contrary evidence the same quality scrutiny as supportive evidence. No required number or share of negative findings. A missing measurement is a gap, not a negative result; a single counterexample need not refute "usually".

## Per-study notes

Record a stable study ID and the research question it addresses, plus:

- Claim supported, with original URL and citation.
- Population/sample, task/setting, intervention/version, comparison and study/data date.
- Design, outcome/denominator, result and meaningful uncertainty.
- Strength within the study; relevance and transfer limits for this brief.
- Important counterresults, competing explanations and missing information.
- Interests/funding, access level and versions or cross-stream duplicates.

Clearly distinguish the source's observations from your interpretation. Unknown details remain unknown. Do not combine different outcomes into a general effect size or split one study into apparent independent corroboration.

Save the scan to the specified scratch file. Include compact search/access notes and actual counts. Return a summary of at most 200 words with the main answer, strongest challenge, consequential gap and file path. The parent will consolidate the evidence before removing scratch files.
